WHAT THE PLAN COVERS

Supplied models. No quota.

Plus covers unlimited completed requests for the service’s qualified subscription models. Existing service limits for concurrency, rate, request size, and timeouts still apply.

A cancelled or unpaid renewal keeps access until the end of the period you already paid. It does not silently spend wallet credit.

Unlimited usage

Completed requests on the qualified subscription catalog are included. There is no monthly request quota and no usage overage charge.

Tax included

The price is CAD $25.00 a month. Stripe collects that amount with tax included in the price.

Separate from RCH

Plus does not mint RCH or add US dollar wallet credit. Basic plans and wallet top-ups stay available on their own.

The success page does not activate the plan. Stripe notifies the REACH service. Open REACH Studio and refresh your account to see Plus. This website never stores your card. Your sign-in session stays in this browser tab only. Return to Login after payment to recheck access; the checkout return page alone does not grant access.
