ABOUT SIGNALREACH

Good tools should
bring things together.

SignalREACH is an independent, open-source project built around a simple idea: your models, your context, and your work should connect.

THE IDEA BEHIND THE NAME

A shorter distance
between idea and action.

REACH stands for RAG Endpoint & AI Chat Host. The project brings together a relay, a control panel, an editor extension, a desktop bridge, and a standalone agent workspace.

Different surfaces. A shared purpose: make AI part of the work, rather than another place to move it.
