Go a little deeper
What is SignalREACH?

SignalREACH stands for RAG Endpoint & AI Chat Host. The project includes REACH Studio, a SimpleRAG control panel and relay, a VS Code extension, and a desktop tray bridge.

Can I use my own model or endpoint?

Yes. REACH Studio and the VS Code extension are bring-your-own-key: connect your own OpenAI, Gemini, or DeepSeek key, or a local Ollama, LM Studio, or Lemonade server, through any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Available models and usage limits depend on your provider. See the endpoint guide.

Do I need to leave my editor?

No. The project includes a VS Code extension for chat and coding inside the editor. Studio is a separate workspace for people who prefer a standalone desktop app.

Is the project open source?

The repository is available under the MIT license. AI providers, hosted endpoints, and optional services may have their own access requirements and charges; open-source software does not mean unlimited free inference.

Is this website connected to my AI account?

The default preview is a local demo. Turn on Try LFM3 VL to build a website with your signed-in REACH account. Only your generated project files and prompt are shared with the model when you send a request; your account access and usage limits apply. The builder does not accept uploads or read computer files.

What does REACH Subscription Plus include?

Plus is CAD $25.00 a month, tax included, with unlimited completed requests on the qualified subscription catalog. It has no monthly quota and no usage overage. RCH and US dollar wallet credit stay separate. See the plan and subscribe with Stripe.
