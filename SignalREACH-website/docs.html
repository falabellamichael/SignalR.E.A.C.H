AUTOMATIONSEveryday work, fewer repeated steps

Give REACH a task.
Let the steps follow.

An automation is a repeatable workflow: a series of steps that AI carries out toward a result. Instead of asking for each small action separately, describe the job, provide the right information, and tell REACH what needs your review.

For a business email, that could mean finding a client’s message, understanding the request, preparing a reply, and showing it to you before sending. You give the direction; REACH helps with the work in between.

How to use automations in REACH.

  1. Connect the tools the task needs.

    In Studio, open Settings → App Connections and connect your work account. For email, reading and sending have separate permissions. You can also start with text you paste into chat when you only need a draft.

  2. Describe the result in everyday language.

    Say what to work on, which information to use, and what a good result looks like. Include the tone, the recipient, and whether you want a draft or a message sent after review.

  3. Run the task and review the result.

    REACH uses the tools available to the conversation to work through your request. Read the proposed email and approve sending when you are ready. A connected account and permission to send are required for a real email.

  4. Reuse the instructions.

    When the task comes up again, use the same instructions with the new client, conversation, or project details. Start with one useful workflow and refine it as you go.

Try a request like this.

Find the latest email from [client] about [project]. Summarize what they need, then draft a friendly reply asking for [next step]. Show me the recipient, subject, and full message for review. Do not send until I approve.

When does it run? This guide shows a workflow you start in chat. Reusing a prompt does not create a recurring schedule. Scheduled or unattended work needs a separately configured scheduling setup.
